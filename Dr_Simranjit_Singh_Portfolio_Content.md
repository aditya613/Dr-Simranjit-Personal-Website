## 2. Professional Biography

**Paragraph 1:**
Dr. Simranjit Singh is an Associate Professor in the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at Punjab Engineering College (Deemed-to-be University), Chandigarh, India, where he has served since May 2023. He holds a Ph.D. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Thapar Institute of Engineering and Technology (T.I.E.T.), Patiala (2011–2014), an M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from Punjabi University, Patiala (University Medalist, 2020–2023), and an M.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering, also from T.I.E.T. (2008–2010). He began his academic career at Punjabi University, Patiala, in December 2011, rising through the ranks of Assistant Professor before moving to PEC Chandigarh. Between July 2016 and July 2017, he completed a prestigious postdoctoral fellowship at The Institute of Optics, University of Rochester, NY, USA, under the Raman Fellowship of the University Grants Commission (UGC), Government of India, where he conducted research on all-optical information security under Prof. Govind P. Agrawal.

**Paragraph 2:**
Dr. Singh's research spans optical fiber communication, information security, and antenna design, with a particular focus on hybrid optical amplifiers, passive optical networks, and MIMO antennas for 5G/6G applications. He has authored more than 100 peer-reviewed publications — including 73 SCI-indexed journal papers with a cumulative impact factor of over 108 — alongside 3 books/book chapters, 1 Indian patent, and dozens of international conference papers. He has been named among the Top 2% Most-Cited Scientists in the world by Stanford University and Elsevier in both 2021 and 2025. As Principal Investigator or Co-PI, he has secured more than ₹3.9 crore in research funding from agencies including SERB, DST, MeitY, UGC, ANRF, and RUSA. He is a Fellow of IETE, a Senior Member of IEEE, and holds editorial positions with SPIE Journal of Optical Engineering, IETE Journal of Research, DRDO's Defence Science Journal, and IET Electronics Letters/IET Journal of Engineering. Beyond research, he has guided 5 completed and 6 ongoing Ph.D. scholars, coordinated PEC's DoT-sponsored 5G Use Case Lab (ranked #1 among 100 labs nationally in 2025), and served as President of PEC's Entrepreneurship & Innovation Cell (IIC), reflecting a career that bridges academic excellence with institutional leadership and industry engagement.
